# Managing Steps & Products in Shopify

## 🎯 New Dynamic System

The bundle builder now uses **Shopify Blocks**, giving you full control to add/remove steps and products directly in the Theme Customizer!

---

## 📝 How to Add/Remove Steps

### In Theme Customizer:

1. **Go to**: Theme Customizer → Bundle Builder section
2. **Click**: "Add block" button
3. **Select**: "Step"
4. **Configure**: Step title (e.g., "Select Your Pack")
5. **Reorder**: Drag blocks up/down to change order

### To Remove a Step:
- Click the step block → Click "Remove block"

---

## 🛍️ How to Add/Remove Products

### In Theme Customizer:

1. **Click**: "Add block" button
2. **Select**: "Product"
3. **Set Step Number**: Choose which step (1, 2, 3, etc.)
4. **Select Product**: Choose from your Shopify products
5. **Reorder**: Drag to change product order

### To Remove a Product:
- Click the product block → Click "Remove block"

## 📱 Quick Tips

**Adding Multiple Products:**
1. Add one product block
2. Set step number
3. Select product
4. Repeat for each product

**Changing Product Order:**
- Just drag the product blocks up/down!

**Removing a Step:**
- Products assigned to that step will still show
- Just change their step number or remove them
